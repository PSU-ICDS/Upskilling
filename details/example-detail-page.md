## What it is

Anthropic Learn is a collection of free courses and guides on using large language models (LLMs). It covers how to write effective prompts, how the Claude family of models behaves, and how to build simple applications and workflows on top of a model. The material is applied and example-driven rather than heavily theoretical.

## Who it is for

People who are new to working with AI assistants and want a practical on-ramp. No programming is required for the prompting material. The building-with-AI sections assume some comfort with code, so a non-technical reader can stop before those and still get value.

## What you will get out of it

A working mental model for what an AI assistant can and cannot do, a set of concrete prompting techniques you can apply the same day, and a sense of when it is worth wiring a model into a larger workflow. If you have only ever typed casual questions into a chat box, this is likely to improve the quality of what you get back.

## Tips and gotchas

The material centers on one vendor's tools, so treat it as a deep example rather than a neutral survey, and pair it with Penn State's [AI Comparison Table](https://ai.psu.edu/ai-services/ai-comparison-table) when choosing what to actually use. For any university work, confirm the tool and account are approved for your data sensitivity before putting real data into it. Most of the introductory material takes an afternoon or less.
