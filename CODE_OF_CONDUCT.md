# Community guidelines

This repository is a shared, public space for the Penn State community. A few expectations keep it useful and welcoming.

## Be constructive

The point of this repository is honest, helpful signal. A `Do Not Recommend` review is welcome when it is fair and explains why. Critique resources, not people. Reviews should describe your own experience, not attack an author, a vendor, or another reviewer.

## Be respectful and inclusive

Faculty here come from every discipline and every level of technical background. Assume good faith. Avoid jargon where plain language works, and never make someone feel unwelcome for being new to a topic. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks have no place here.

## Keep it relevant and appropriate

Add resources that genuinely help people upskill in AI. Do not use the repository for advertising, self-promotion unrelated to learning, or content that violates Penn State policy or the law.

## Respect copyright

Link to resources rather than re-hosting copyrighted material you do not own. Only upload files you created or have permission to share. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Reporting a problem

If you see content that breaks these guidelines, or you have a concern about someone's conduct, email `ICDS-FACULTY-UPSKILLING@lists.psu.edu`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may decline contributions, to keep the repository useful and respectful.

This repository also operates under applicable Penn State University policies.
